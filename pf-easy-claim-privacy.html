Privacy Policy — PF Service Easy Claim

Effective date: 28 October 2026

PF Service Easy Claim is an independent Android utility application offered by Common e Services. It provides convenient links and an in-app browser to access official Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) websites.

Independence notice: This is not an official EPFO or Government of India application. It is not affiliated with, approved by, or endorsed by EPFO or any government authority.

1. Information and website access

The app opens official EPFO websites in an embedded browser. If you enter a UAN, password, OTP, or other information on an EPFO webpage, you are interacting with that website. The handling of information by EPFO is governed by its own policies and practices. The app does not require a separate PF Service Easy Claim account.

Embedded websites may use cookies, local storage, and other browser technologies to provide their services. Data entered into external websites may be transmitted to those websites.

2. Advertising

The app integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements on its home screen. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and other data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where applicable, personalization. Applicable consent choices may be presented depending on region and configuration. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

3. External and affiliate links

The app may include links to Amazon and Flipkart. Some links may be affiliate links, meaning the app operator may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you. These third-party services have their own privacy practices. The app may also offer a WhatsApp contact shortcut; using it opens a third-party service governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Technical information

Websites and advertising services accessed through the app may receive technical information needed for connectivity and security, such as IP address, device/browser information, and request details. The app may retain browser-related data locally as needed for website functionality. Information collected or retained can vary by app version and device configuration.

5. Security

We aim to provide convenient access to official portals. No internet transmission or application can be guaranteed completely secure. Always check that you are on an official EPFO website before entering credentials, and never share OTPs with other people.

6. Children's privacy

The app is intended for users seeking provident fund services and is not designed for children. Advertising and third-party websites may apply their own age-related policies.

7. Your choices

You can choose not to open external links. You can manage app permissions and clear app storage through Android Settings; clearing storage may remove browser cookies and locally saved site data. For information held by EPFO, Google, WhatsApp, Amazon, or Flipkart, contact the relevant provider.

8. Policy changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app, services, or legal requirements. The effective date above will be revised when the policy changes.

9. Contact

For questions about this app or this privacy policy, email info@commoneservices.online or visit www.commoneservices.online.
